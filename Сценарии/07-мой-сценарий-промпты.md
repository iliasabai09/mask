# Вариант 7. Промпты для Flow

Схема: **фото → видео по фото**. Сначала генерируем стартовый кадр по фото-промпту, выбираем лучший, потом во Flow запускаем видео из этого кадра (Frames to Video) с видео-промптом. Формат 16:9.

## Постоянные описания (не менять между кадрами)

- **Улица:** узкая жилая улица маленького городка 80-х, двухэтажные деревянные дома с крыльцами, один фонарь мигает, мокрый асфальт, у бордюра тёмный прямоугольный ливневый сток. Через дорогу дом с тёмным окном на втором этаже: это будущий дом Маски.
- **Прохожий:** мужчина лет 40, короткие тёмные волосы, щетина, усталое лицо, бежевый плащ, тёмные брюки, чёрный зонт.
- **Клоун:** описание из паспорта Пеннивайза, без имени.

## Как держать единый вид
1. Когда кадр 1 получится, прикладывайте его как референс улицы к кадрам 2–4.
2. Когда в кадре 2 получится прохожий, сохраните его во Flow как ингредиент/персонажа и используйте в 3–4.
3. Когда в кадре 5 получится клоун, сохраните его как персонажа (например «Dancing Clown»), он понадобится дальше.

## Кадр 1 · Шарик вылетает из стока и зависает
**Фото**
```
Cinematic film still. Night, heavy rain on a narrow 1980s small-town residential street: old two-storey wooden houses with porches, across the street a house with a single dark upstairs window, one flickering street lamp, wet asphalt reflecting the light, rainwater rushing along the curb into a dark rectangular storm drain opening. Low angle near the curb. A single red balloon is just emerging from the storm drain. No people. Cold blue-grey color grade, the red balloon is the only bright color. Photorealistic, 35mm, moody horror lighting.
```
**Видео**
```
The red balloon slowly rises out of the storm drain, floats straight up and stops in mid-air at about the chest height of an adult, then hangs completely still, its white string dangling straight down, unaffected by the wind and rain. Static camera with a very slow push-in. Sound: heavy rain, water gurgling into the drain, distant thunder. No music.
```

## Кадр 2 · Появляется прохожий
**Фото**
```
Cinematic film still. Night, heavy rain on a narrow 1980s small-town street, wet asphalt, one flickering street lamp, a dark storm drain opening in the curb. A man in his 40s walks along the wet sidewalk under a black umbrella: short dark hair, stubble, tired face, beige trench coat, dark trousers. In the foreground a red balloon hangs motionless in mid-air at chest height above the storm drain, its string dangling. The man has just noticed it and slows down. Medium-wide shot. Cold blue-grey color grade, the red balloon is the only bright color. Photorealistic, 35mm.
```
**Видео**
```
The man slows down, stops and stares at the floating red balloon, tilting his head in confusion. Rain drums on his umbrella. The balloon stays perfectly still in the air. Slow push-in toward the man's puzzled face. Sound: heavy rain on the umbrella, footsteps on wet asphalt.
```

## Кадр 3 · Тянет шарик, а он не поддаётся
**Фото**
```
Cinematic film still, medium close shot. Night, heavy rain. A man in his 40s with short dark hair and stubble, wearing a beige trench coat, holds a black umbrella in his left hand and with his right hand grips the white string of a red balloon hanging motionless in mid-air at chest height. His face shows surprise and disbelief. Behind him a wet 1980s small-town street with a flickering street lamp and a storm drain at the curb. Cold blue-grey color grade, red balloon as the only bright color. Photorealistic, 35mm.
```
**Видео**
```
The man tugs the string, trying to walk away with the balloon, but the balloon does not move at all, as if it is fixed in the air. He pulls harder, leaning his whole body back, his shoes slipping on the wet asphalt, then tries again, bewildered. The balloon stays perfectly still, defying physics; only the string stretches taut. Slight handheld camera. Sound: heavy rain, his strained breathing.
```

## Кадр 4 · Глаза в стоке
**Фото**
```
Cinematic film still. Low angle at curb level, night, heavy rain. In the foreground the dark rectangular storm drain opening in the curb, pitch black inside. In the background a man in a beige trench coat stands holding the string of a red balloon that hangs motionless in the air, looking up at it in confusion, his black umbrella tilted. Wet asphalt reflecting a flickering street lamp. Cold blue-grey color grade, red balloon as the only bright color. Photorealistic horror, 35mm.
```
**Видео**
```
The man stands still, puzzled, staring up at the balloon. Inside the pitch-black storm drain in the foreground, two amber-yellow eyes suddenly light up and glow, looking slightly in different directions, staring at the man. Slow push-in toward the drain. Sound: heavy rain, then a soft wet giggle from inside the drain.
```

## Кадр 5 · «Нравится шарик?..»
**Фото**
```
Cinematic film still, extreme close-up inside a dark storm drain at night, rainwater dripping past the opening. The face of a sinister clown, lit only by faint street light: cracked white greasepaint, high domed bulbous forehead, tufts of curly orange-red hair, thin red vertical lines running through the eyes down to the corners of the mouth, red nose, red upturned lips, protruding buck teeth, glowing amber eyes looking slightly in different directions, the edge of a grey ruffled collar. He smiles sweetly. Photorealistic horror, cold blue-grey color grade with warm amber eyes, 35mm.
```
**Видео**
```
The clown slowly tilts his head at an unnatural angle, his smile widening to show his buck teeth, a drop of drool on his lip, and whispers in a sweet, giggly voice: "Do you like the balloon?.." Then he freezes, staring straight at the camera. Very slow push-in. Sound: rain and dripping water.
```
**В монтаже:** сразу после фразы резкий звук тревоги и чёрный экран. Голос клоуна заменить озвучкой из ElevenLabs.
